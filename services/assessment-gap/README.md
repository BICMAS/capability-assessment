The assessment-gap service stores attempts for knowledge, situational judgement, self-assessment, and manager assessment, and calculates the current level for a person. AI interview and simulation percentages are kept on the explanation and have no writers yet. The level is the mean of the available scoring percentages divided by 25. Self-assessment is stored and left out of that mean. Each explanation keeps the source percentages, mean, divisor, required level, gap, framework version, and a provisional status. A manager override stores the actor, reason, previous value, and new value, and publishes level.overridden for the audit log.
